06 Packaging

Opened like a postcard.

Every order arrives in a box printed with a scene from the shore: striped sides, a beach drawn on the lid, tied with twine and a kraft tag, and sealed with the palm.

Lift the lid and it becomes a postcard: Greetings from LA PLAGE. Dressed for the tide. Your piece waits folded inside, the patch facing up. A day by the sea should start with something to open, not just something to wear.
