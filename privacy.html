Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

LA PLAGE ("we", "us") is a beachwear label based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. By using the site you acknowledge this policy. Where you give us your consent (for example to receive emails) we rely on it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

LA PLAGE, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Contact for anything in this policy: our contact page.

2. What we collect

  • Mailing list. Your email address, the date and time you signed up, where you signed up from, and your consent and unsubscribe status.
  • Account. Username, name, Gmail address, phone number, delivery address and a password (stored by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in readable form).
  • Pre-orders. The pieces, sizes, colours and quantities you order, your delivery details, any note you add, totals and order status. We do not take card details on this site; payment is arranged with you directly.
  • Technical data. Our hosts and service providers may log your IP address, browser type and request times for security and to keep the site running.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

3. Why we use it

  • To email you when pre-orders open (20 November 2026) and about future drops, only if you ticked the consent box. This is marketing, and you can stop it at any time (see section 6).
  • To create and run your account, take and fulfil your pre-order, contact you about it, and deliver it.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse and meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions with legal effect.

4. Who we share it with

Only with the services that help us run the shop, under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database): stores accounts, pre-orders and the mailing list.
  • EmailJS (sending through our Gmail account): sends welcome and pre-order emails.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • Delivery partners: receive your name, phone and address to deliver your order.

We may also disclose data where the law or a competent authority requires it, or to protect our rights. These providers may process data outside Vietnam (including in Singapore, the United States and the EU). By giving us your data you agree to that transfer, and we choose providers that apply reasonable safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Mailing list: until you unsubscribe. After you unsubscribe we keep only your email address and unsubscribe status, so we do not email you again.
  • Accounts and pre-orders: while your account is open and for as long afterwards as needed for delivery, warranty, accounting and legal obligations (up to 10 years for financial records).

6. Your choices and rights

Unsubscribe. Every email we send carries an unsubscribe link, and you can also use the unsubscribe page or send us a message. We stop marketing emails promptly and in any case within 3 working days. Service emails about a pre-order you placed may still be sent.

You may also ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, or to receive a copy. We will reply within a reasonable time (30 days at most). Send us a message from the address you used. If you are in Vietnam, these rights come from Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on personal data protection; if you are in the EU or UK, from the GDPR / UK GDPR, and you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies and storage

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Signing in uses your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

8. Security

Data is held in Google's infrastructure behind access rules that let only you (and our admins) see your account and orders, and mailing list addresses can be read only by our admins. No online service is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; material changes will be announced on the site or by email.
